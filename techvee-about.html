Background
How a British IT specialist ended up in Turbenthal.

I'm British, I've been based in Switzerland for many years, and I hold a Swiss C Residency Permit — which means I work here with full Swiss employment rights, pay Swiss taxes, and am fully accountable under Swiss law. I'm not a remote contractor who happens to have Swiss clients.

I started Techvee GmbH because I kept seeing the same pattern: Swiss small businesses paying either too much for IT support they didn't need, or too little for IT support that wasn't delivering. The middle ground — experienced, direct, honest IT consultancy at a fair price — seemed underserved.

I describe myself as an IT specialist who started a company, not a businessman who happens to know IT. That shows in how I work. I'm more comfortable talking about Azure architecture or nDSG compliance than discussing pipeline metrics or brand positioning. I'm interested in solving your actual problem, not in impressing you with process.

My clients tend to value directness. If I can't help you, I'll tell you. If what you're considering is a bad idea technically, I'll say so. If someone else is better placed to solve your problem, I'll point you to them. That's not a sales technique — it's just how I think IT consultancy should work.

How I work
What working with Techvee actually looks like.

You talk to me

Not an account manager, not a helpdesk, not a junior who'll escalate to someone else. When you're a Techvee client, you have my direct contact details and I respond personally.

I tell you what I find

If your IT is in good shape, I'll say so. If there are issues, I'll explain them clearly and give you a realistic picture of cost and effort to fix. No manufactured urgency, no upselling.

I scope work precisely

Project work is quoted as a fixed price against a defined scope. You know what you're paying before work starts. No open-ended billing, no scope creep surprises at the end.

I understand the Swiss context

Swiss compliance, MWST, Treuhänder relationships, Swiss banking requirements, FINMA — I work in this environment every day. No learning curve at your expense.

I respect your time

I respond on the same business day. I don't put you on hold while I "raise a ticket". I don't send you generic troubleshooting guides before actually engaging with your problem.

I stay current

AI, Copilot, nDSG, EU AI Act, new Azure services — I follow these closely because they affect my clients. I bring relevant updates to you rather than waiting to be asked.
