Do you work with businesses outside Canton Zurich?

Yes — most of my work is done remotely. For on-site visits I cover Canton Zurich and neighbouring cantons without travel charges. For other locations I charge travel at cost.

?

Do you work in English?

Yes, English is my first language. I also work in German for clients who prefer it, though my German is functional rather than native.

?

What's the minimum commitment for a retainer?

Three months. This gives enough time to learn your environment and deliver meaningful value. After three months you can continue month-to-month or adjust the scope.

?

What happens to unused hours in a retainer month?

They roll over to the following month. Unused hours accumulate for up to three months, then expire. I'll flag if you're consistently using far more or less than your included hours.

?

How quickly do you respond?

Retainer clients get a same-business-day response guarantee. For ad-hoc support, I typically respond within a few hours during business hours.

?

Can you work alongside our existing IT team?

Absolutely — this is a common arrangement. I complement in-house IT with specialist skills or capacity they don't have, rather than replacing what's already working.
