Microsoft Copilot
What Copilot actually does — and what it costs.

Microsoft Copilot is an AI assistant integrated directly into the Microsoft 365 apps your team already uses. It can draft emails in Outlook, summarise Teams meetings, generate first drafts in Word, build formulas in Excel, and create presentations in PowerPoint — using your organisation's own data.

Deployment & rollout
Switching on Copilot is the easy part.
A successful Copilot deployment requires preparation, governance, and training. Most failed deployments happen because organisations skip these steps.
1

Readiness assessment

Review your existing M365 configuration, licence tier, data residency settings, and security posture. Identify any blockers before you commit licences.

2

Governance framework

Define acceptable use, set up sensitivity labels, configure data loss prevention policies, and document how Copilot interacts with your data — especially for regulated data categories.

3

Pilot group rollout

Start with 5–10 motivated users across different roles. Gather feedback on real use cases, identify quick wins, and build internal champions before wider rollout.

4

Training & adoption

Role-specific training on prompting effectively — an hour with your team on how to get good results from Copilot is worth more than a policy document nobody reads.

5

Ongoing optimisation

Monitor usage data, identify underutilised features, and iterate. Copilot capabilities update frequently — I keep your team ahead of what's new.

Swiss AI compliance
AI governance matters in Switzerland too.
The EU AI Act has indirect implications for Swiss businesses with EU clients or operations. The nDSG governs how AI-processed personal data must be handled. Getting ahead of this now is far less painful than retrofitting.
⚖️

EU AI Act & Swiss businesses

The EU AI Act applies to AI systems deployed in the EU — including systems developed by Swiss companies for EU clients, or Swiss businesses using AI-enabled EU services. High-risk AI categories require documentation, testing, and human oversight. I can advise on whether your use cases fall within regulated categories.

🔒

nDSG & AI data processing

When AI tools process personal data — which Copilot does when it reads emails and documents — this is subject to the nDSG. Data subject rights apply. Processing must be documented. Cross-border AI processing requires adequate protection. I help you document and govern this correctly.
