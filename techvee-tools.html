Retainer vs. in-house: what does senior IT support really cost?

Most Swiss SMBs need a few hours of senior IT attention a week, not a full-time person. This compares four ways of covering those hours — two from me, two by hiring. The retainer and ad-hoc figures are my published prices; the hiring figures are yours to adjust.

Retainer: CHF 600 per month includes 5 hours; extra hours CHF 130. Ad-hoc: CHF 150 per hour, 1-hour minimum. Both exclude MwSt. A 42-hour week is used for the part-time percentage. Finding an experienced person willing to work 5–20% is, in practice, rare — which is why the full-time figure is the realistic comparison for most firms.
